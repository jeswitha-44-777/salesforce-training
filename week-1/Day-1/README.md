# 🚀 What is CRM?

CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management.

It helps companies:
- manage customer information
- track communication
- improve sales
- automate business processes
- provide better customer support

---

# ☁️ What is Salesforce?

Salesforce is a cloud-based CRM platform used by companies to:
- manage customers
- track sales
- automate workflows
- build business applications

---

# ❓ Why Companies Use Salesforce

Companies use Salesforce because it:
- centralizes customer data
- improves team communication
- automates repetitive tasks
- tracks sales performance
- improves customer support

---

# 📘 Salesforce Objects

## Account
Represents a company or organization.

Example:
- Amazon
- Infosys
- College

---

## Contact
Represents a person associated with an account.

Example:
- Student
- Employee
- Customer

---

## Opportunity
Represents a possible business deal or process.

Example:
- Admission process
- Product purchase
- Service request

---

# 🔄 Business Workflow

Lead → Contact → Opportunity → Customer

Explanation:
1. Lead = Interested person
2. Contact = Identified person
3. Opportunity = Potential deal
4. Customer = Final converted buyer
