# Day 1 Notes – Salesforce Basics

## What is CRM?
CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management.

It helps companies:
- manage customer data
- track communication
- improve sales
- provide better customer support

---

## What is Salesforce?
Salesforce is a cloud-based CRM platform used by companies to manage:
- sales
- customers
- marketing
- support services
- business automation

---

# Why Do Companies Use Salesforce?

Companies use Salesforce because it:
- stores all customer data in one place
- improves communication between teams
- automates repetitive tasks
- tracks sales and performance
- improves customer service

---

# Real Problems Solved By Salesforce

## 1. Scattered Customer Data
Problem:
Customer information is stored in different places.

Solution:
Salesforce centralizes all customer data.

---

## 2. Missed Follow-Ups
Problem:
Sales teams forget calls or meetings.

Solution:
Salesforce provides reminders and task automation.

---

## 3. Poor Team Communication
Problem:
Different departments work separately.

Solution:
All teams can access shared customer information.

---

## 4. Manual Repetitive Work
Problem:
Employees spend time on repeated tasks.

Solution:
Salesforce automates emails, reports, and workflows.

---

## 5. Difficulty Tracking Sales
Problem:
Managers cannot track performance properly.

Solution:
Salesforce dashboards and reports provide live analytics.

---

## 6. Slow Customer Support
Problem:
Customer issues are not tracked properly.

Solution:
Salesforce stores complete interaction history.

---

# Objects in Salesforce
Objects are similar to database tables.

Examples:
- Student
- Contact
- Account

---

# Records in Salesforce
A record is a single entry inside an object.

Example:
A student’s details inside the Student object.

---

# Difference Between Admin and Developer

## Admin
- Configures Salesforce
- Uses no-code tools
- Manages users and reports

## Developer
- Writes code
- Builds custom applications
- Uses Apex and Lightning components

---
